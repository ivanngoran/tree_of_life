Good to know

Questions people ask.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Coaching is forward-looking and works with you as someone already whole and capable. It is not treatment for a mental health condition. If something calls for a therapist, I will say so and help you find the right support.

What happens on a free fit call?

We spend about forty-five minutes talking through where you are and what you need. There is no pitch. If I am the right fit, we talk about next steps. If I am not, I will point you somewhere better.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Coaching and much of the consulting work happen remotely, and I work in person where it makes sense.

How long do engagements run?

It depends on the work. Coaching ranges from a single session to a longer arc. Consulting can be a few hours, a scoped project, or an ongoing retainer. We settle on the shape together.

What does it cost?

Flat fees, shared with you upfront. No ranges and no surprises. You know what happens and what it costs before we begin.

Do you work with individuals or organizations?

Both. Individuals come for coaching and programs. Organizations come for consulting and team workshops. The through-line is the same.

Whole-person coaching and practical HR and business consulting. Strong systems and people who feel seen are the same work.

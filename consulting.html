Case study

What this looks like in practice.

Draft for your approval, client anonymized

Building HR infrastructure for a growing care provider.

The situation

A supported-living provider was growing fast on systems that had not kept up. Hiring, onboarding, and time tracking lived in scattered places, and a few compliance gaps carried real wage and hour risk.

What we did

Built onboarding and hiring workflows, corrected worker classification and pay practices, closed the gaps between scheduling and payroll, and replaced a manual matching process with a proper system.

The result

A cleaner, defensible HR foundation the team can run day to day, with the biggest risks addressed before they became findings.
